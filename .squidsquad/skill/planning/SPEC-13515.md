# SPEC-13515 — `status:blocked` (owned-but-parked) status

**Task:** #13515 (approved, doc-first). **Phase:** 1 (spec). **Gate (AC6):** PM reviews this spec BEFORE Phase-2 code.
**Origin:** this session's #13464 + #13457 both held `status:in-progress` — a *legitimate* block-and-continue (implemented #13464, blocked on a PM-authored CQ AC, parked it, continued to #13457), indistinguishable from a true double-pickup bug because `in-progress` is overloaded.

## 1. The problem: `in-progress` is overloaded
`status:in-progress` today means BOTH "actively working" AND "parked/blocked-on-another-party." So:
- PM pipeline-sentinel cannot tell a genuine double-pickup (bug) from legitimate block-and-continue without reading comments.
- A role legitimately holding 2 in-progress (one active, one parked-blocked) looks identical to a broken agent that grabbed two tasks.

## 2. The status model
| Status | Ownership | Actively worked now? | Wakes whom |
|--------|-----------|----------------------|------------|
| `in-progress` | assignee | YES | — |
| **`blocked` (new)** | **assignee (RETAINED)** | **NO — parked, blocked on another party** | — (owner keeps it) |
| `pending-*` | HANDED OFF (transfers) | n/a | the new owner |

`blocked` = "the assignee still OWNS this task but has parked it because it is blocked on another party (a PM-authored AC, a dependency PR, a human decision); it is NOT being actively worked right now." It KEEPS ownership (unlike `pending-*`, which transfers) and signals not-active (unlike `in-progress`).

Working name `status:blocked`; alternative `status:parked`. **PM decision needed:** confirm the name (`blocked` vs `parked`). Rest of spec uses `blocked`.

## 3. Legal transitions (Phase-2 code)
- `in-progress -> blocked` (assignee parks: blocked on another party, keeps ownership).
- `blocked -> in-progress` (assignee resumes: unblocked).
- Authority: `_assignee` (only the owning agent parks/resumes its own task).
- ALL existing transitions unaffected (regression test, AC3).

## 4. Doc edits (Phase 1) — proposed wording
### 4a. L1 shared Soul — `references/roles/SOUL.md` "Never Stop While Work Is Pending" (§ at L47)
The first paragraph (L49) covers only handoffs that LEAVE your lane (`pending-*`, ownership transfers). Add the still-owned-but-blocked case. Proposed insert after the handoff sentence:

> When work stays in your lane but you are **blocked on another party** (a PM-authored AC, a dependency PR, a human decision), you still own it — **transition it to `status:blocked`** (owned-but-parked), *not* left at `in-progress` (which reads as actively-working) and *not* `pending-*` (which would hand off ownership) — then immediately continue to your next item. Parking to `blocked` is the sanctioned form of block-and-continue; leaving a blocked task at `in-progress` is what makes a legitimate park indistinguishable from a double-pickup.

### 4b. `event-mode-contract.md:83` Case D "Mid-task, event arrives"
Mirror the same parking rule in the event-reaction path: a still-owned task blocked on another party is transitioned to `blocked`, not left in-progress.

### 4c. Canonical taxonomy — SKILL.md label taxonomy + `tracker.py` header docstring
Document `status:blocked`: semantics (owned-but-not-active), legal transitions (§3), authority (`_assignee`).
