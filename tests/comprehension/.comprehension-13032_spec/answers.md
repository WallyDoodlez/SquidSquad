### Q-CQ1
You must end your session so your process exits — not keep it running while idle. The contract is explicit: after emitting `ack-stop` with `result="deploy-halted"`, you must "**halt and end your session so your process exits**: stop all agent work (do NOT pick up new work, do NOT enter the improvement-scan cool-down loop)... Then **immediately invoke `/quit` to terminate your session**, exactly as the exit-42 self-restart path does." It clarifies: "'Halt' here means **end the session**, not merely pause work — the same way exit-42 means `/quit`, not idle." So the specific action is: invoke `/quit` to terminate the process (and do NOT `ack-cursor` the deploy-signal).

### Q-CQ2
Terminating the process is load-bearing because of the harness's singleton guard. Per the contract: "This step is load-bearing: the harness respawns you by spawning a **fresh** `claude` process, and its singleton guard refuses to spawn while your old process is still alive — so if you halt *without exiting*, the respawn no-ops and you keep running the **stale, pre-recompose** `CLAUDE.md` (the whole point of the deploy is defeated)." So: the harness respawns you by spawning a fresh `claude` process; if your old process is still alive when it tries, the singleton guard refuses to spawn, the respawn no-ops, and you continue running the stale pre-recompose `CLAUDE.md` — defeating the entire purpose of the deploy.

### Q-CQ3
After you exit, the harness runs the deploy sequence off your now-exited session: "ensure-on-`main` → `git pull` → `compose.py deploy <your alias>` → commit → push → advance your cursor → **respawn a fresh process** (possible only because you exited above)." Reading the newly composed `CLAUDE.md` happens in a **new session**, not your halted one: "Your fresh session boots reading the newly committed `CLAUDE.md` (it does NOT recompose at boot — AGENT-RUNTIME §8.2)." Any nudges that arrived during the deploy window are delivered after the fresh session is `ready` and drained normally.
